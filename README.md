## 🔎 Analysis Performed

This project covers the following stages of SQL-based data analysis:

### 1. Database Exploration

Explored the database structure, tables, columns, and metadata using `INFORMATION_SCHEMA`.

### 2. Dimension Exploration

Explored customer countries and the product hierarchy, including categories, subcategories, and products.

### 3. Date Range Exploration

Analyzed the historical period covered by the sales data and examined customer age ranges.

### 4. Measures Exploration

Calculated key business metrics, including:

- Total revenue
- Total quantity sold
- Average selling price
- Total orders
- Total products
- Total customers
- Customers with orders

### 5. Magnitude Analysis

Analyzed business metrics across different dimensions, including:

- Customers by country
- Customers by gender
- Products by category
- Average product cost by category
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by customer

### 6. Ranking Analysis

Identified top and bottom performers, including:

- Top 5 products by revenue
- Bottom 5 products by revenue
- Top 10 customers by revenue
- Customers with the fewest orders

This section also demonstrates the use of SQL ranking functions such as `RANK()`.

### 7. Change Over Time Analysis

Analyzed sales performance over time using:

- Year and month
- `DATETRUNC()`
- `FORMAT()`
- Revenue
- Customer counts
- Quantity sold

### 8. Cumulative Analysis

Applied window functions to calculate:

- Running total revenue
- Average price over time
- Cumulative performance trends

### 9. Performance Analysis

Performed product-level year-over-year analysis using:

- `LAG()`
- `AVG() OVER()`
- `CASE`
- Previous-year sales comparison
- Above/below average performance

Both subquery and CTE approaches were explored.

### 10. Data Segmentation

Created business-oriented segments for:

- Products based on cost ranges
- Customers based on lifespan and spending behavior

Customer segments include:

- VIP
- Regular
- New

### 11. Part-to-Whole Analysis

Analyzed the contribution of each product category to overall revenue using window functions and percentage calculations.

### 12. Customer Report

Built a customer-level analytical report containing:

- Customer information
- Age and age group
- Customer segment
- Lifespan
- Recency
- Total orders
- Total sales
- Total quantity
- Total products
- Average order value
- Average monthly spend

### 13. Product Report

Built a product-level analytical report containing:

- Product information
- Category and subcategory
- Cost
- Market duration
- Product lifespan
- Recency
- Revenue-based segmentation
- Total customers
- Total orders
- Total revenue
- Total quantity sold
- Average selling price
- Average order revenue
- Average monthly revenue
- Gross profit
